Questions buyers ask first
What is the typical lead time?

Standard products ship in 2–3 weeks ex-works from Anand, Gujarat. Made-to-order products and custom chemistries are quoted case by case.

What documentation do you provide?

Technical datasheet and certificate of analysis per lot. Extractables information, cleaning validation support and product-change notification are available on request.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We supply worldwide ex-works or via your preferred forwarder, with commercial invoice, packing list and technical documentation. Resins ship in 20% ethanol at ambient temperature.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on product, grade, pack size, quantity and destination. Bulk volumes of 5 L and above and custom pack sizes are priced individually.
