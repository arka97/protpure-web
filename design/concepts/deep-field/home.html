Questions from the bench.

Do you offer evaluation packs?

Yes. Paid evaluation packs are typically 5–25 mL, or a 1 mL pre-packed column. The cost is credited against your first bulk order.

What documentation comes with the resin?

A technical datasheet and certificate of analysis per lot. Extractables information, cleaning validation support and product-change notification are available on request.

What is the typical lead time?

Standard products ship in 2–3 weeks ex-works from Anand, Gujarat. Made-to-order products and custom chemistries are quoted case by case.

Can I replace an imported resin directly?

We recommend a side-by-side evaluation under your existing SOPs. Our specifications are benchmarked against typical market specifications; our resin screening service can run the evaluation for you.
