06 / Product FAQ

Before you qualify.

Are these drop-in replacements for imported resins?

Specifications are benchmarked against typical market specifications. We recommend a side-by-side evaluation under your existing SOPs; our resin screening service can run it for you.

What documentation comes with each lot?

Technical datasheet and certificate of analysis per lot. Extractables information, cleaning validation support and product-change notification are available on request.

How are resins packaged and stored?

Resins are supplied as a slurry in 20% ethanol in sealed HDPE containers. Store at 4–30 °C, away from freezing. Shelf life is stated on the certificate of analysis.
