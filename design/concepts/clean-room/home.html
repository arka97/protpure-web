06 / Before you begin

Questions
buyers ask first.

Ordering, evaluation and documentation.

How do I evaluate a resin?

We quote paid evaluation packs, typically 5–25 mL or a 1 mL pre-packed column. Scientist support and documentation are included. The cost is credited against your first bulk order.

What documentation do you provide?

A technical datasheet and certificate of analysis per lot. For regulatory filings, additional documentation is available under a confidentiality agreement.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We supply worldwide ex-works Anand or via your preferred forwarder, with commercial invoice, packing list and technical documentation. Resins ship in 20% ethanol at ambient temperature; no cold chain is required.
